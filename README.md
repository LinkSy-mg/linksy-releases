# Linksy for Android

Your devices, connected. Share files, clipboard text and more between your own devices, straight over your Wi-Fi: no account, no cloud.

## Download

- **[linksy.apk](https://github.com/LinkSy-mg/linksy-releases/releases/latest/download/linksy.apk)**: works on every Android phone.
- [linksy-arm64.apk](https://github.com/LinkSy-mg/linksy-releases/releases/latest/download/linksy-arm64.apk): smaller, for 64-bit phones.
- [linksy-armv7.apk](https://github.com/LinkSy-mg/linksy-releases/releases/latest/download/linksy-armv7.apk): smaller, for 32-bit phones.

Every release and its notes: [Releases](https://github.com/LinkSy-mg/linksy-releases/releases).
